# Hummingbird fixed-bound ablation: all 40 cells complete

The fixed change from one to two outstanding completion events increases
background goodput by about **15% in both native C and host-uBPF**. It does
not establish unchanged foreground protection: the prespecified joint
protection test remains inconclusive. Under BurstGPT-derived arrivals, SLO
attainment decreases by 0.44 percentage points for C and 0.56 points for BPF.
This is an executor/pacing tradeoff, not a new BPF policy or a BPF-only gain.

The frozen joint win test requires the LC p99 ratio's upper interval bound
to be at most 1.01, SLO difference's lower bound at least −1 pp, and BE
ratio's lower bound above 1. None of the depth comparisons passes that test.
Their BE gains are clear, but unchanged LC protection is not established.
At bound 1 under BurstGPT, BPF has a small measured BE loss (0.242%);
the other BPF/C BE intervals include zero change. No equivalence claim follows
from an interval containing zero, nor is safe full-stack overhead isolated.

## Claim boundary

The bound counts host-issued, not-yet-retired completion events, not physical
GPU queue occupancy. HP arrival stops further submissions; it cannot cancel
already submitted work. These results support a bounded-pipeline tradeoff in
this compatibility port and a matched native/JIT comparison. They neither
reproduce the full original Hummingbird system nor establish hardware
preemption guarantees, close the older fixed-GPreempt gap, or demonstrate a
new scheduling algorithm. The four-panel workload plot is complete as a
separate artifact; paper placement is not completed by this measurement report.
